
# Simple P-nut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

## Ingredients
- 2 slices of bread
- 2 tablespoons of peanut butter
- 1 tablespoon of jelly or jam

## Instructions
1. Lay the two slices of bread flat on a clean plate.
2. Use a butter knife to spread the peanut butter on one slice.
3. Clean the knife or use a new one to spread the jelly on the other slice.
4. Press the two slices together with the spreads facing inward.
5. Cut the sandwich in half with a knife.

## Done When
- The peanut butter and jelly sandwich is done when two slices of bread are coated evenly with peanut butter and jelly, pressed together firmly, and cut completely in half.
